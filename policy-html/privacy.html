Privacy Policy — DRAFT

Draft for internal review only. Not legal advice. Must be reviewed by a lawyer licensed in British Columbia before the public launch of Shopping Paradise For Deals. This policy is written to align with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and substantially similar provincial legislation.

Effective date: [to be set at launch] Operator: Shopping Paradise For Deals, Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. 100% Canadian owned and operated. Contact: Adam, our AI support agent, through the Help centre on the Site.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored hashed).
  • Buyer order information: shipping address, order history.
  • Seller information: business name, business address, country, payout details (handled by Stripe; we do not store bank account numbers).
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Technical information: device type, browser, IP address, pages visited — used for security and improving the service.

2. Why we collect it (PIPEDA purposes)

  • To create and manage accounts.
  • To process orders, payments, shipping, and returns.
  • To pay Sellers and issue tax documents.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the Marketplace secure.
  • To communicate about orders, accounts, and policy changes.
  • With your consent, to send marketing communications (you can opt out anytime).

We collect only what a reasonable person would consider appropriate for these purposes.

3. Consent

By creating an account or placing an order, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure described here. You may withdraw consent (e.g., by deleting your account), subject to legal and contractual requirements such as retaining transaction records.

4. Who we share it with

  • Sellers: Buyers' names, shipping addresses, and order details are shared with the Seller fulfilling the order — for that purpose only.
  • Stripe: payment processing and Seller payouts, under Stripe's own privacy policy.
  • National postal services (e.g., Canada Post, USPS, Royal Mail): delivery name and address, only for the postal service fulfilling the order.
  • Legal: where required by law, court order, or to protect against fraud or harm.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Where data lives and how it's protected

Data is hosted with our service providers (currently Render, United States region) and Stripe. We use encryption in transit and at rest, hashed passwords, and access controls. No method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We keep account and transaction records as long as your account is active and for 7 years afterward for tax, accounting, and dispute purposes, then delete or anonymize them.

7. Your rights

Under PIPEDA you may request access to your personal information, ask for corrections, and ask how it has been used or disclosed. Contact Adam through the Help centre — we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for login, cart, and security. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

9. Changes

Material changes will be announced on the site at least 14 days before taking effect.
