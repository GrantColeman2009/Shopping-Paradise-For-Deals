This agreement, together with the Terms of Service and the Returns & Disputes Policy, governs selling on the Marketplace.

1. Eligibility

  • Sellers must be legitimate businesses or sole proprietors able to fulfil orders as described.
  • Sellers from any country may sell; every listing publicly shows the Seller's country and each product's made-in country.
  • One account per business. We may request business verification documents.

2. Fees [rates PROPOSED — awaiting operator approval]

  • Monthly subscription: $14 CAD per month, billed in advance. [PROPOSED]
  • Per-sale marketplace fee: 4% of the order subtotal. [PROPOSED]
  • Payment processing (Stripe) is included in the per-sale fee.
  • Fees are deducted from payouts. The subscription is non-refundable except where required by law.
  • We will give at least 30 days' notice of fee changes.

3. Payouts

  • Payouts are issued on the 14th and 28th of each month at 10:00 AM Pacific Time, covering cleared funds, minus fees, refunds, and chargebacks.
  • Payouts go to the Seller's connected Stripe account. Sellers must maintain a valid Stripe connection and keep payout details current.
  • We may hold payouts during fraud reviews or unresolved disputes.

4. Listings

  • Listings must be accurate: title, description, images, condition, price, and stock levels.
  • Shipping is FREE for Buyers in Canada and the USA: build your estimated domestic shipping cost into the listed price — the Buyer pays no separate charge.
  • International shipping is charged at checkout: set your per-item international Standard shipping rate when listing. Buyers outside Canada/USA choose Standard or Express (1.8× the Standard rate) at checkout, and that amount is added to their order total.
  • State the product's made-in country truthfully for every product.
  • State a realistic dispatch time (time to ship after order).
  • Prohibited: counterfeit, stolen, illegal, hazardous, or IP-infringing items; misleading pricing; directing Buyers off-platform to avoid fees.

5. Fulfillment and shipping

  • Ship within the stated dispatch time via your home country's national postal service (e.g., Canada Post for Canadian Sellers, USPS for US Sellers, Royal Mail for UK Sellers). Private couriers are not used.
  • Provide tracking where available. Mark orders shipped promptly so Buyers can follow progress.
  • Late or unshipped orders may be cancelled and refunded at the Buyer's request, and repeated failures may lead to suspension.
  • Insurance: every shipment is insured up to (never more than) your national postal service's maximum coverage. If a shipment is lost or damaged, shipping insurance claims are handled by the Marketplace (Adam) — you must provide reasonably requested documentation (proof of shipment, photos, invoices) within 5 business days. You do not deal with the carrier's claims process yourself.

6. Returns and disputes

Sellers must honour the Returns & Disputes Policy: accept eligible returns, respond to Buyer messages within 2 business days, and cooperate with platform mediation. Chargeback and refund amounts are deducted from the Seller's payouts.

7. Taxes

Sellers are responsible for their own income-tax and GST/HST (or equivalent) obligations. We collect and remit sales tax at checkout where required and provide Sellers with transaction records.

8. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or terminate a Seller account for policy violations, fraud, chronic late shipment, unresolved disputes, or chargeback abuse. Either party may terminate with 30 days' notice; fees owed remain payable and eligible payouts will still be issued.

9. Independent businesses

Sellers are independent contractors, not employees, agents, or partners of the Marketplace.

10. Contact

Seller support: Adam, our AI support agent, through the Help centre on the Site.
