1. Our promise

If your order arrives damaged, defective, materially not as described, or never arrives, you are entitled to a remedy: replacement, repair, or refund. Nothing here limits your rights under applicable consumer protection law.

2. Change-of-mind returns

  • Buyers may return unused, unopened items in original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.
  • Change-of-mind return shipping is paid by the Buyer unless the Seller offers otherwise.

3. Damaged, defective, or not-as-described items

  • Report within 30 days of delivery (with photos where possible).
  • The Seller must offer a prepaid return label or a replacement/refund without requiring a return, at the Seller's choice.
  • Return shipping for these cases is always at the Seller's expense.

4. Items that never arrive

  • If tracking shows no delivery within 10 business days past the estimated delivery date (or 30 days after shipment if no estimate was given), the Buyer may request a full refund.
  • Lost-in-transit claims are handled by the Marketplace (Adam) under the shipment's carrier insurance (see Terms of Service §6.5). The Seller must provide requested documentation within 5 business days.

5. How to start a return or report a problem

  1. Contact the Seller through your order page, describing the issue.
  2. The Seller must respond within 2 business days.
  3. If the Seller doesn't respond or you can't agree, open a dispute with us through the order page within 45 days of delivery.

6. Dispute mediation

  • We review the order, messages, tracking, and photos.
  • We aim to decide within 10 business days.
  • Outcomes may include: full or partial refund, replacement shipment, or a finding in the Seller's favour.
  • Our decision is final for Marketplace actions (refunds issued through the platform, Seller account standing). Either party retains the right to pursue legal remedies independently.

7. Chargebacks

If a Buyer files a chargeback with their card issuer instead of using this process, we will supply the order evidence to the processor. Abuse of chargebacks may lead to account closure.

8. Non-returnable items

For health, safety, or legal reasons, certain items can't be returned (e.g., opened personal-care items, perishable goods, digital downloads). Sellers must clearly mark items as non-returnable on the listing; even then, damaged/defective/not-as-described protections still apply.

9. Contact

Returns and dispute support: Adam, our AI support agent, through the Help centre on the Site.
